# @on.auto/activity

The organization's activity in Auto Runtime: what the playbooks of an org did that a person may want to see, kept for the whole org so a client shows it with one read however many playbooks the org holds, and what each caller has seen of it. It owns one fact, `activity_seen`, four keyed projections of the ledger and two org operations, `list_activity` and `mark_activity_seen`, defined on the application layer, [`@on.auto/operations`](../operations). [The organization's activity](../../docs/reference/http.md#the-organizations-activity) in the HTTP reference documents the routes in full.

## The fact

A caller marks rows seen with one `activity_seen`, recorded through `OrgWriter` on `org/<org>/activity/<id>`, the stream of what that caller has seen. A caller's id, such as `person:<subject>`, holds characters a stream name does not, so `<id>` is the UUID version 5 of the caller's id; the raw id is the context's `by`, as every fact of an org carries it with `at` (`ActedContextSchema`).

| Field     | What it holds                                                                                                     |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `items`   | The `new` rows the caller has seen, each `{ address, at }` as the row gives them, `at` in ISO 8601; may be empty  |
| `through` | Left out, or `{ at, playbook, key }`, the place in the activity everything at or before which the caller has seen |

`ActivitySeenSchema` is its schema. Its fixture is `src/facts/0.56.0/activity_seen.json`, listed in `recorded-facts.json`, and the server reads it among its known facts.

## The projections

Each fact changes one row of a projection, so a run that succeeds, which both makes its own row and resolves its definition's broken row, is kept in two tables. Times are whole milliseconds since 1970-01-01T00:00:00Z, so a read pages and compares on them. A resolved request or broken definition stays in its table, closed, and is never served: every read and count asks for `open = true`.

| Projection            | Folds                                          | Keyed by                                                                             | What a row holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `activity_1`          | A playbook's `runs` and `definitions`          | `runs/<run id>` for a run, `definitions/<type>/<name>/<version>` for a version saved | A run no other run started, from its `run_started`, which keeps who started it, its trigger and output shape, `listed` once its `run_succeeded` lands; and each `definition_created` and `definition_updated`, listed at once, with `change` `created` or `updated`. A run a workflow's call started keeps no row                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `activity_requests_1` | A playbook's `runs`                            | The run's id                                                                         | Who started the run, from `run_started`; `open` from a `run_deferred` whose request takes an answer, a step's included, with the first line of its message (at most 200 characters), its answer schema (at most 8 KiB as JSON, otherwise none), whom it waits on and when it expires; closed by `request_answered` or the run's ending                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `activity_broken_1`   | A playbook's `runs` and `definitions`          | `<type>/<name>`                                                                      | The current version of the definition, from `definition_created` and `definition_updated`; `open` from a `run_failed`, or a `run_rejected` that is neither `cancelled` nor `unanswered`, of a run no other run started at the current version or later, with its status, run, `detail` (at most 200 characters) and trigger; closed by a `run_succeeded` at that version or a later one, by a new version saved and by `definition_retired`. A failure of an older version, a cancelled run and a request nobody answered write nothing |
| `activity_seen_1`     | An org's `activity` streams, `scopes: ['org']` | The caller's id, the context's `by`                                                  | Where the caller marked everything seen through, `through_at`, `through_playbook` and `through_key`, which only moves forward, and `seen_at`, when it last moved; and `marked`, a JSON list of the 1,000 items the caller marked last after `through`, newest first, each `{ address, at }`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

A request waits on the lower-cased address its party names where the party holds an `@`, and on `any_member` otherwise. `activityProjections` lists the four for a composition; the server opens its ledger with them.

## The operations

Both are org operations served over HTTP alone, permitted by `org:read` or `playbook:read`; `activityOperations` lists them for a catalog.

| Operation            | Kind    | Route                 | Input                                                                               | Answer                                             | Rejections of the handler                |
| -------------------- | ------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| `list_activity`      | query   | `GET /activity`       | `kind`, `playbook`, `mine`, `address`, `since`, `limit` (50, at most 200), `cursor` | `{ rows, has_more, next_cursor, counts, through }` | `invalid_input`, `not_found`             |
| `mark_activity_seen` | command | `POST /activity/seen` | `items` (at most 200), `through`, `address`                                         | `{ counts, through }` after the mark               | `invalid_input`, `forbidden`, `conflict` |

## The reads

`list_activity` folds the org's register of playbooks, `org/<org>/playbooks`, which no keyed projection can fold since its name has no id: it gives the active playbooks the caller may access and a `new` row for each of the newest 100 changes that created or updated a playbook. For each of those playbooks it reads the open rows of the three tables of a playbook through `OrgReader.readPlaybookRows`, newest first by `at`, and merges them with the playbook rows newest first by `(at, playbook, key)`. A cursor is the base64url of the JSON `{ at, playbook, key }` of a row. A read orders by `at` alone in SQL, reading strictly before the cursor's `at` and after the `at` of `since`, and the rows that share one of those times, or the oldest time of a full read, whole, so that the order of rows of one time is decided in code, the same on SQLite and PostgreSQL, whose collation orders text otherwise.

The counts are over every active playbook the caller may access, whatever the filters: the open requests that wait on `any_member` or on the `address` read, each of them for the local developer; the open broken definitions; and the listed `new` rows after the caller's `through`, less the caller's own and those the caller marked, counted through `OrgReader.countPlaybookRows` with `{ column: 'at', after }`. A caller who has never marked anything reads as if `through` were 7 days before the read. A row is seen when its `by` is the caller, when the caller marked its address, or when it is at or before `through`.

## Testing

`pnpm --filter @on.auto/activity test` runs every test beside the code, against the in-memory ledger of `@on.auto/operations/testing`, with 100% coverage per file. Each fold transition has a test in `src/projections`, and `src/operations` drives both operations through the dispatcher over a scenario of two playbooks (`src/testing/scenario.ts`). The server's `src/activity` drives both routes over HTTP on a temporary SQLite ledger.
