# @on.auto/content

The content model every store of the runtime shares: the bytes a playbook keeps by their SHA-256 digest beside its messages, the port that keeps and reads them, the refusals of a put, the reading of a stream in chunks through the digest, the check of what a store reads back, and the digest itself. It is a package of its own because the ledger, the object store of [`@on.auto/object-storage`](../object-storage/README.md) and the operations all build on it, and it needs nothing of the runtime but `effect` and Node's digest; the operations bind the port to the playbook of a call (see [Recorded content](../operations/README.md#recorded-content)).

## The port

A tool call's arguments and a tool's answer are kept whole, whatever their size, beside the messages rather than in them, since every decision on a stream and every projection rebuild reads its facts back, and so is any content a capability keeps by its digest. `RecordedContent`, the port the ledger implements, keeps content under a `ContentOwner`, `{ org, playbook }`, the playbook it is kept for, which a `PlaybookAddress` of `@on.auto/operations` is, takes bytes as a stream and computes their digest itself (`src/content/recorded-content.ts`):

- `put(playbook, { stream, mediaType, expected? })` reads a web `ReadableStream<Uint8Array>` in chunks of `mostContentChunkBytes`, 1 MiB, digests it with SHA-256 as it passes and measures it, keeps it under the playbook and its digest, and answers `ContentKept`, `{ sha256, bytes, mediaType }`. `expected`, `{ sha256?, bytes? }`, is what the caller asserts of the bytes: a stream whose digest or size is not the one expected is refused with `ContentRefused` of `because: 'not_its_digest'` or `'not_its_size'`, carrying as `found` the digest and size the port read, so a route words the mismatch without digesting the bytes again, and a stream that fails before its end with `because: 'stream_failed'`, carrying the stream's own error as `cause`; each carries a `detail` sentence, so a route answers every refusal as one `invalid_input`. Nothing of a refused put is kept: no read finds it, and the ledger deletes the chunks it wrote, as its README says.
- A put of a digest the playbook already holds keeps the content it holds, with the media type it was first kept with, and answers that one, so an identical answer is kept once however often it comes and a second media type never replaces the first. Each content a store keeps has a head, which carries its size, its first media type and when it was last kept, the milliseconds of Effect's `Clock` at the put, and a put of a digest the playbook holds, whole or with its digest expected, moves that time to its own, so a removal of what was kept before a time leaves a content a put has just kept again.
- `get(playbook, sha256)` answers a `StreamedContent`, `{ stream, bytes, mediaType }`, or nothing for a digest the playbook does not hold; a read whose bytes are not its digest's fails as a defect before it hands over the last of them (`notTheBytesOfItsDigest`). `verifiedStream(sha256, bytes, source)` is that check for every adapter: it reads the stream an adapter has, digests and counts each piece, hands over the piece that reaches the size the adapter knows only once the size and the digest are checked, and fails on a stream that ends short of the size, goes past it, or gives a piece that is not bytes.
- `getPart(playbook, sha256, asked)` answers a part of a content, as a player asks for one: `asked`, an `AskedRange`, is `{ start, end }`, the first byte and the last, counted from 0, or `end: undefined` to the end. It answers a `ContentPart`, `{ size, mediaType, part }`, the size of the whole and its media type, and as `part` the `StreamedPart` of the range within the content, `{ start, end, bytes, stream }`, its last byte the content's last where the range runs past it, or no `part` where the range starts at or past the end or is no range of bytes, a start that is not a whole number of 0 or more, an end before the start or one between two bytes, which `rangeWithin(asked, size)` decides for every adapter, whoever asks it (`src/content/content-parts.ts`); it answers nothing for a digest the playbook does not hold. A part cannot be checked against the digest of the whole, so it is held to its length alone: `measuredStream(sha256, bytes, source)` fails a part that ends short of its length or goes past it, without the piece that did, as a defect (`notTheBytesOfItsPart`), and a reader that needs the bytes checked reads the whole. In memory a part is a slice of the bytes kept, the ledger reads the chunks the range covers alone, cutting the first and the last, and an object store asks for the range of the object alone, as their READMEs say.
- The port removes nothing: a content may be held by more than one record of its playbook, since two records that keep identical bytes keep one content, so it is removed only by the ledger, `Ledger.removeUnheld` of `@on.auto/operations`, where no fact of its playbook holds it and the hour after it was last kept has passed, answering `removed`, `held` or `fresh`, an `UnheldRemoval`, as the [ledger README](../ledger/README.md#the-holders-of-content) says. A reader that holds a stream of the content as it is removed is left to its own failure: the ledger fails it as a defect at the first chunk it had not read yet, `The content kept under <sha256> is missing its chunk <n>`, and on an object store the read already begun ends as the store answers it.
- A content kept outside the ledger, on an object store, is kept by `keptOutside(heads, store)` (`src/content/kept-outside.ts`), over two ports: `ContentHeads`, the heads the ledger keeps in its own database, and `ContentStore`, the store of the bytes. `ContentStore` is `{ stage, holds, read, readPart, delete, sweepContent }`: `stage(playbook, content)` reads the stream through the digest into an upload of the store's own and checks what is expected, answering a `StagedContent`, `{ taken, placed(mediaType), discarded() }`, whose `placed` puts the bytes under their digest with the media type it is given, only where no object is there, and `discarded` deletes the upload; `holds` looks whether the bytes of a digest are there, `read` streams them through `verifiedStream`, `readPart` streams a part of them, `delete` deletes them, and `sweepContent(before)` deletes the uploads of puts that began before that time and never finished. `ContentHeads` is `{ kept, touched, marked, cleared, unmarked, read }`: `kept(playbook, sha256, head, keptAt)` writes a head, or moves the time of the one that stands, and answers `new`, `standing` with that head, or `removing` with the time of the mark a removal set on it; `touched` moves the time of a head that stands and answers it, `removing`, or nothing where there is none; `marked(playbook, sha256, { before, now, staleBefore })` marks, where nothing holds the digest, a head kept before `before` and not marked, or marked before `staleBefore`, or, where no head stands, writes a head of the mark alone, so bytes the store holds with no head are removed too, and answers `{ marked: true, since }`, or `{ marked: false, answer }` with `held`, `fresh`, or `removed` for a head another removal marked; `cleared(playbook, sha256, since)` deletes the head that mark names; and `unmarked(playbook, sha256, since)` lets go of that mark where it still stands, deleting a head of the mark alone; and `read(playbook, sha256)` answers the head that stands, its size and its media type, or nothing for none or a head of a mark alone. A read, whole or of a part, streams the bytes from the store with the media type of their head, so it answers the media type a put answers wherever the bytes were placed and with whatever media type, and that of the bytes where no head stands. A put stages its bytes, keeps their head, and places them where the head is new or its bytes are not there, discarding its upload otherwise; a put with its digest expected moves the time of the head that stands, looks that the store holds its bytes and reads its stream through the digest alone. A removal marks the head, deletes the bytes, and clears the head, and lets go of its mark where the delete fails, so a put after it keeps the content at once; a put that meets the mark polls every `removalPollMs`, 100 ms, until the head is gone and then keeps the content as new, and past `removalWaitMs`, 5 s, it is refused as `unavailable`, `The content kept under <sha256> is being removed, so it is not kept now; keeping it again in a moment keeps it whole`, which a caller asks again; a mark older than `removalLastsMs`, ten minutes, is the mark of a removal that stopped, which the put, or a removal after it, finishes. So a put either keeps the head before the removal marks it, and the removal answers `fresh`, or after the removal deleted the bytes, and writes them again, with two limits: a removal that stalls more than ten minutes between its mark and its delete is taken for one that stopped, so a put after those ten minutes places the bytes again and the stalled delete, when it goes on, takes them, leaving a hold that names bytes the store no longer holds; and a put whose bytes take more than the hour to reach the store after it kept their head meets a removal that takes the head, so the bytes it places stand with no head, read and held as any, until a removal of their digest deletes them.

## The digest and the streams

The digest is Node's, behind the one module of the repository for it (`src/content/content-digest.ts`), which serves the whole repository, the hex SHA-256 of a license key, of a fixture or of an answer the server keeps among them: the digest of what is in hand, `sha256Of(text or bytes)`, is the one-shot form, `hash('sha256', content, 'hex')`, and the digest of a stream, `sha256Digest()`, is the incremental one, `createHash('sha256')`, which takes the stream in parts; a text is digested and kept as its UTF-8, so its digest is the SHA-256 of the text. `takenInChunks(stream, chunkBytes, take)` reads a stream through the digest in chunks, answering its digest, size and number of chunks or a `stream_failed` refusal, `requireExpected(expected, taken)` refuses a digest or a size that is not the one expected, and `leftUnread(stream)` cancels a stream no one reads; the ledger, the object store and the in-memory content keep through the first two, and the ledger and the object store read back through `verifiedStream`. `textToKeep(text, mediaType)` makes the content of a text to put, `contentOfText` and `contentOfBytes` a `StreamedContent` of a text or of bytes in hand, and `textOfContent(content)` reads one back as text, failing as a defect on bytes that are not UTF-8.

## Testing

`@on.auto/content/testing` keeps recorded content in memory: `memoryContentStore()`, a `ContentStore` of bytes, `memoryContentHeads(heldIn?)`, the heads, which take whether the ledger holds a digest from `heldIn`, and `memoryRecordedContent()`, the port over both, which the tests of other packages take, as the in-memory ledger of `@on.auto/operations/testing` keeps its content through `keptOutside` over both; `pausableStore(store)` wraps a store so that a test pauses, and then releases or fails, its next delete or its next placing, `pausedDelete()` and `pausedPlace()`, which the suites of a removal meeting a put run with, `settledWithin(work, ms)` tells whether a promise settled within that time, and `defectOf(effect)` answers the defect an effect died with; and `keptTextOf(content, playbook, sha256)` reads a kept text back as text.

`recordedContentBehaviour(entry)` is the behaviour suite of the port, in `src/testing`: a content of no bytes to 64 MiB kept and streamed back, a part of 2,500,000 bytes read from its first byte, across chunks, to the end of an open range and of one that runs past it, and no part of a range past the end, of one that starts before the first byte or of one that ends before it starts, a text kept once with its first media type in its playbook alone, a content put again whole or with its digest, the refusals of a wrong digest, a wrong size and a stream that fails, wrong bytes put with the digest of a content held, and a wrong put racing a right one. Its times are set with `at(moment, effect)`, which runs an effect on Effect's `TestClock` at that moment. An adapter runs it with a `ContentEntry` whose `aStore()` answers `{ content, elsewhere, unnamedLeft }`: the port, the port of a second client of the same store, and how many pieces of puts no kept content names the store holds, which is 0 after every test. This package runs it on memory, the ledger on SQLite and PostgreSQL, and `@on.auto/object-storage` on its fake bucket and on a real S3-compatible server, through heads in memory; the removals of content are the ledger's, so their suites are those of `@on.auto/operations/testing`, run on every ledger. `src/content/kept-outside.test.ts` runs `keptOutside` over the heads and the bytes in memory: a removal of a content kept before its time and a put of it after with its own media type; `held`, `fresh` and `removed` for a digest never kept; the time of a head moved by a put whole and with its digest; a head that stands without its bytes placed again with its media type; a put during a paused delete, whole and with its digest, that waits and keeps the bytes; a put refused as `unavailable` once it waited 5 s; two removals stalled after their marks finished by a put and by a removal, which take back no mark as they fail at last; a slow removal whose head another removal took over, which clears nothing; the bytes the store holds with no head removed while a put that meets the removal waits; and a removal whose delete fails letting go of its mark, and of the head of its mark alone. The samples it is made of are exported with it: `alpha` and `beta`, two playbooks of one org, `octets` and `json`, two media types, `generated(bytes, seed)` and `generatedStream`, bytes made the same for a seed, `answerOfAbout(bytes)`, a JSON answer of about that size, `readBack` and `readThrough`, which digest what a read streams, `deliveredBeforeFailing(stream)`, how many bytes a failing stream handed over, `brokenAfter(bytes)`, the content of a stream that fails once it handed over that many bytes, and `resultOf`, `refusalOf` and `present`, which run an effect for its value, its failure or its value present.
